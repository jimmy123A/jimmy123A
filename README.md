## Hello — I'm Dimitris.

I'm a product-focused full-stack engineer building software where digital
products meet physical space: interactive floor plans, 3D scenes, mapping
tools, native capture, and the systems behind them.

Most of my work lives across the **[Planpoint](https://planpoint.io)** ecosystem,
where I move between product design, spatial interfaces, geometry, APIs, cloud
services, payments, and cross-platform development.

## Focus

I build end-to-end spatial products across web and native platforms — from
geometry, pathfinding, and 2D/3D rendering to media pipelines, SDKs, payments,
and cloud infrastructure. Right now, I'm focused on making architectural data
easier to capture, understand, edit, and move between devices and immersive
experiences.
